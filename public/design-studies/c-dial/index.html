Study C: Dial

A calm focus timer. The talk is a ring of arcs, one per card, and you turn the dial down to your limit. A run of show beside it says when each card starts. On a phone, the slider sits in a dock under your thumb.

References

  • Apple Watch timer and Activity rings: a ring that is both the progress and the control.
  • Forest and Flow (focus timer apps): a night palette and one thing in focus. Calm rather than loud.
  • TV and theatre run-of-show sheets: start times down the left edge, so you know when each card begins.
  • Heuer and Casio stopwatch faces: a ring of ticks with the elapsed time large in the centre. Sora’s wide, round figures echo that.
  • Headspace and Oura night modes: soft pastels on deep blue instead of neon on black.

Motion

  • Slider drag. The handle follows the pointer around the ring. Kept arcs wind and unwind with a 200ms ease, and the centre number ticks over without moving.
  • Card cut. Its arc in the inner ring fades out as the following arcs rotate back to close the gap. On the outer ring the same card turns into a dashed coral arc.
  • Card shortened. The arc shrinks in place and changes from peach to lilac. In the run of show, the start times below it count down to their new values.
  • Reduced motion. Arcs and times switch to their new state without rotating or counting.

Printed cue sheet

The screen is dark, but the cue sheet prints on white: peach numbers become black circles, the run-of-show times stay in the margin, and the dial is left out. That means a second print stylesheet to keep in sync.

Verdict

Strengths

  • The most striking first impression. The dial makes a great demo moment.
  • Run-of-show times answer a question the other two don't: when does each card start?
  • The phone dock keeps the time control under your thumb while you scroll the talk.

Weaknesses

  • A circular slider is harder to make accessible. It needs a real range input behind it, which this mock already has.
  • A dark theme is less like the printed cue cards, and needs a separate light print style.
  • Dragging around a small ring (280px on a phone) is less precise than a straight track. The dock covers that case.

Implementation cost: High

Custom SVG dial with pointer math, an accessible fallback, a sticky two-pane layout, a phone dock, and a separate print theme. Roughly twice A's work.
