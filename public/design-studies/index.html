Keep the Point: three visual directions

Each direction shows the same real sample talk at 1:45 (2:38 → 1:42, one card shortened, one cut). The sliders in every preview work: each 5-second step uses the app's real selection result. Open a study for the cue cards, states, type, colour, motion and cost.

A: Tape measure

Time as a length. The talk sits on a yellow ruler and you drag a red limit line; anything past it hangs off the end.

Cost: medium Open study A

B: Highlighter notebook

Your talk is the page. One list, marked like study notes: pink strike for cut, yellow highlight for the short version, reasons in the margin.

Cost: low to medium Open study B

C: Dial

A calm night-blue focus timer. Turn a ring of card arcs down to your limit; a run of show says when each card starts.

Cost: high Open study C
